Drawing scale and model assumptions
  • Ground floor calibrated to the 21.90 m overall length and the 17.00 m main west-wall dimension. Upper floor calibrated to the printed dimension chains, including the 15.45 m chain and 12.90 m depth, then registered to the west wall, north wall and stair core. Small inconsistencies in the scans were rounded.
  • Ground floor datum follows FFL 9.800. Garage follows FFL 9.450, 350 mm lower. Upper floor assumed 3.00 m above the ground floor. Modelled wall heights are 2.75 m downstairs and 2.70 m upstairs.
  • Typical external and internal walls use the printed 250 mm and 100 mm thicknesses. Door and window sizes, sill heights, stair winders and railing details are approximate where the drawings are unclear.
  • The roof follows the four elevations as a simplified tiled hip with secondary gables and lower roofs. The elevation’s 8.40 m maximum guides the ridge height above approximate ground level. Eaves, roof junctions, brick colour, frames, cabinetry and finishes are illustrative.
